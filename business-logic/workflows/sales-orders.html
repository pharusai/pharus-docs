Sales Orders

Workflows for sales orders, their line items, timeline notes, attached documents, and the three SO-side master-data tables (order channels, order status types, fulfillment status types). SOs are the demand side of finished goods: every line is a SKU the org has promised to deliver. On creation and through the fulfillment lifecycle, SO workflows write DEMAND, ALLOCATE, and CONSUME entries to the FG ledger, mirroring how PO and WO workflows feed it.

Components composed across this domain: SalesOrders, FinishedGoodsInventory, Customers, Products, Locations, Documents.

Status enums (seeded from order_status_types): Received → Entered → Shipped → Invoiced → Paid plus terminal branches (Rejected, In-Dispute, Completed). Fulfillment status (seeded from fulfillment_status_types): Pending → Partial → Fulfilled, derived from the ledger by default but also editable.
