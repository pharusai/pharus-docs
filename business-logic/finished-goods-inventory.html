Finished Goods Inventory

Workflows for manual movements of finished goods — transfers, adjustments, disposals. Parallel structure to Materials Inventory; same shape, different ledger, different event types. Indirect entries (RECEIVE from FG-target PO receipts, PRODUCE from WO completion, DEMAND / ALLOCATE / CONSUME from SO lifecycle) are written by their owning workflows.

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory, Products, Locations.

Transfer ref convention: TFR-FG-xxx, auto-generated on the outbound leg.

Writes​

1. Create transfer between locations​

Triggered from the Finished Goods Inventory Transfers page → "New transfer".

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory, Products, Locations.

Steps:

  1. Verify the SKU exists and is active.
  2. Verify both source and destination locations exist, are active, and distinct.
  3. FinishedGoodsInventory.recordTransfer({ skuId, locationId: sourceLocationId, quantity: -quantity, eventDate, notes }) — outbound leg. Auto-generates a TFR-FG-xxx ref.

Returns: the outbound entry and ref.

Business rules:

  • Mirror Materials Inventory §1: positive user-facing quantity, negative stored on the outbound leg, destination captured on the inbound leg at completion.

Errors:

  • NotFoundError, ValidationError.

2. Complete in-transit transfer​

Triggered from the Finished Goods Inventory Transfers page → "Mark complete".

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory.

Steps:

  1. FinishedGoodsInventory.listEntriesByRef(ref) — confirm the outbound exists and no inbound yet.
  2. FinishedGoodsInventory.recordReceipt({ skuId, locationId: destinationLocationId, quantity, ref, eventDate, notes }). unitCost is carried over from the outbound entry's stored unit cost (FG transfers preserve cost; they don't recompute).

Errors:

  • NotFoundError, InvalidTransitionError.

3. Adjust inventory​

Triggered from the Finished Goods Inventory page → "Adjust".

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory, Products, Locations.

Steps: same as Materials Inventory §3, swapping materialId for skuId and calling recordAdjustment.

Errors: NotFoundError.

4. Dispose inventory​

Triggered from the Finished Goods Inventory page → "Dispose".

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory, Products, Locations.

Steps: same as Materials Inventory §4, calling recordDisposal.

Errors: NotFoundError.

Reads​

5. Get finished goods inventory summary​

Triggered from the Finished Goods Inventory page.

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory, Products, Locations.

Steps:

  1. FinishedGoodsInventory.getSummary(filters) — returns { skuId, locationId, onHand, demand, allocated, available }. The FG summary is richer than the materials side because of the DEMAND / ALLOCATE rollups.
  2. Batch-hydrate SKU and location names.

Notes:

  • available = onHand - allocated is the field sales ops actually plan against. The L2 summary computes it.

6. List ledger entries​

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory.

Steps: FinishedGoodsInventory.listEntries(filters). Filters include refLike: 'TFR-FG-%' for the Transfers view.

7. Get ledger entry detail​

Composes: FinishedGoodsInventory.

Steps: FinishedGoodsInventory.getEntryById.
