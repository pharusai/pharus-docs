Access levels​

The Access column indicates the minimum role required to call the workflow:

  • Member. Any authenticated user in the organization. Day-to-day operations: create or update transactional records, read data, manage own profile.
  • Admin. Organization configuration. Invite or revoke users, manage taxonomy records (*-type, *-segment, *-channel, brokers), delete master records, bulk imports, sensitive inventory ops (adjust, dispose), locations.
  • Owner. Destructive organization-level actions. Update or delete the organization itself, change user roles, remove members.
  • Platform admin. Cross-tenant operations exposed through a separate admin client. None today; reserved for future workflows (for example, tenant provisioning, platform-wide audits).
